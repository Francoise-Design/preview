FAQ
Is the lead exclusively mine?
Exclusivity applies to the specific contact and business unit you introduced us to — not the entire company. For example, if you introduce us to the Head of Design at Revolut Business, that introduction is protected for 6 months. It does not block us from independently reaching Revolut's Retail, Crypto, or other divisions, nor from responding to inbound interest from Revolut employees who contact us on their own.
What if I only have a cold contact, no real relationship?
We do not pay for blind email intros or simple name-dropping. We are not running a passive referral program right now — the 10% program is strictly reserved for active Deal Champions who have genuine relationships and can provide internal context.
